Decisions in the mockups to confirm

  1. Where employees see past days. A new "My recent days" list sits under Sign In / Out, with the yellow Request correction button on any day that needs one. "My corrections" is its own page, reached from that list.
  2. Manager reviews in the app. Requests appear on a Corrections page in the Overtime look, each with a read-only evidence panel. HR keeps the full list, direct corrections, settings and the report in wp-admin.
  3. Reset Day in wp-admin. The Sign In / Out Report's "Reset Day" deletes a day's records today, which conflicts with "never change the original". Proposal: keep it for genuine mistakes, but write it to the Audit Log and steer HR to a direct correction instead.
  4. Foreman's menu. A foreman's tab bar shows My sites and Payout in place of Schedule and Leave; their own Sign In stays in the middle.
  5. wp-admin language. The plugin's existing wp-admin pages are still in English on an Arabic site. The new pages are drawn translated, which is how they will ship.
