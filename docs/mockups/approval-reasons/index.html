Why it is with administrators, and requests outside a workflow — mockups

Why a request is with administrators (QA 8): the card says why it came to them, one text per reason (no direct manager, HR or Finance not set, no team or department manager, the approver is no longer active or has no account, the approver made the request, not decided in time with nobody above). A link opens the setting that sends the next requests to the right person, only for those who can change it. Old requests saved before this show a neutral "Administrators decide it". The person who asked sees the reason in My requests, without the link.

Requests outside an approval workflow (QA 6): overtime while its workflow is "Not set yet" and a leave cancellation while leave approvals are off are cards in My approvals for the people who can decide them today, with "No approval workflow", no level and no note (those paths keep none). They count in the menu and on Home. Nothing changes in who decides.
