Shift swaps in My approvals — mockups (3.31.124)

My approvals lists everything that waits for your decision: also a swap a colleague asked you for (accept or decline), whatever week its day is in. The colleague's notification opens it there. After the colleague accepts, the swap goes to the approver as today.

The Schedule page no longer lists requests: one line with the count and a link to My approvals; your own requests are in My requests. With My approvals switched off, the Schedule page keeps today's swap panel. No new setting, no database change.
