Week lock — mockups (Step 1)

From a weekly cutoff (here Thursday 5:00 PM, site time) the coming week's schedule is final and stays final. Managers can no longer change it; people with the new permission "Edit a locked week" (site administrators and EWS Administrator by default) can, and every change is audited. Leave, leave cancellations and swaps are still requested, approved and applied automatically, also on final days. Attendance is not locked: corrections work as today. Off by default; when off nothing changes.

Release 2 (right after): a manager's change on a final day becomes a request to HR with a reason. In release 1 it is refused with a message.
