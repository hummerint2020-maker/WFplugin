Feature Configuration

Week lock
From a fixed time every week, next week's schedule is final and stays final: managers can no longer change it; attendance is still fixed through corrections as today. Off by default.
Cutoff

Every week at this time, next week's schedule becomes final. Site time: Cairo (UTC+3), from WordPress Settings → General.

Now

Next cutoff: Thu 8 Oct, 5:00 PM (the week of 11 – 15 Oct). Weeks before it are final.

Who can edit a final week

Site administrators and the EWS Administrator role.

The "Edit a locked week" permission. Give it to other roles (such as HR) on Roles & Permissions

Requests

Leave, leave cancellations and swaps are requested, approved and applied as usual, also on final days.

Every change written into a final week is recorded in the Audit Log.

Employees

Employees always see a "Final" mark on the week in their schedule.
