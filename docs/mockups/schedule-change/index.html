Schedule change request — mockups (week lock, release 2)

While the week lock is on and this feature is switched on, a manager's change on a day of a final week becomes a request to HR with a reason instead of being refused. HR decides it in My approvals (app) or on the Requests page (wp-admin); the levels are set in Approval Workflows (HR by default). On approval every day that is still as it was is applied and the employee is told; a day that changed meanwhile (a leave, a swap) is skipped. Leave days go through a leave request. Past days up to 14 days back, never in a month closed in Payroll. A waiting request closes after 14 days or when its month closes.

Off by default; off means release 1 exactly. Nothing new in the phone app this release.
