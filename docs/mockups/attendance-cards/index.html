Attendance cards you can open — mockups (3.31.125)

The cards at the top of the Attendance page open a list of who and when: each person and day behind the number, with the sign-in and sign-out times, the minutes late, or where the person was planned to be. A side sheet on a computer, a bottom sheet on a phone.

The list follows the page's week, search, team and branch, and always counts what the card counts. It loads when the card is opened (the first 20, then "Show more"), so a large company does not slow the page. A switch on the Features page, off by default; off, the cards stay as today.
