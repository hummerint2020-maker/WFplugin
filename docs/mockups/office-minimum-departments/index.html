Office minimum by department — mockups (Step 1)

Each department can have its own minimum of people and of managers in the office, checked on its own. The company's minimum stays as it is; a department without a number has no check of its own. A department manager sees their department's numbers; the administrator sees the company and can pick a department. Only a warning, off by default (a department needs a number).
